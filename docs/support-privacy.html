Support privacy.

Updated 18 November 2026 · Applies to the website's Get help chatbot and support ticket requests, not desktop file transfers.

Free guided support

The Get help chat uses a built-in troubleshooting guide, not generative AI. Your questions are transmitted to Droply's Supabase support endpoint to select a prepared answer; they are not forwarded to OpenAI or another language-model provider. Recent chat messages remain in your browser tab until you clear the chat or close the page. Droply's endpoint does not intentionally save ordinary chat messages in its database or include them in application logs, although hosting infrastructure may process connection metadata. Avoid sharing passwords, private keys, file contents or other sensitive information.

Support tickets

When you explicitly select Create support ticket, review your issue summary and press Submit ticket, the name, email address and summary you enter are saved in a separate private Supabase support-ticket table. Only the authorized Droply administrators can review and update the ticket and their resolution notes. Chatbot conversations are not automatically submitted as tickets.

Why we collect tickets

We use ticket information to investigate problems, follow up with you and manage issue resolution. Your ticket is separate from the marketing newsletter and from ordinary Contact us messages. We do not sell your support information.

Abuse protection

Our server uses temporary rate-limit counters derived from protected one-way identifiers to restrict spam. We do not put raw IP addresses into support ticket records. Hosting providers may process connection metadata as part of normal logs.

Access, deletion and replies

Ticket records are kept as long as reasonably needed for support and follow-up, subject to deletion requests and applicable legal requirements. To ask for a copy, correction or removal, contact dev.leonb@gmail.com. Tickets currently reach the team's private admin panel rather than triggering an automatic reply email or a guaranteed response time.

Your choice

Chat and ticket submission are optional. They are separate from the Droply desktop app and do not affect your ability to transfer files.
