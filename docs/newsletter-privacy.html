Newsletter privacy.

Just the updates. Not your data.

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · This notice covers the newsletter signup on the Droply marketing website, not file transfers in the desktop application.

What we collect

When you sign up, we store your email address, the date and time you gave consent, the version of the consent statement, the signup source and whether your subscription is active or withdrawn. We do not ask you to create an account for the newsletter.

Why we use it

With your consent, we use your email address to send occasional Droply release news, product updates and tips. We do not sell subscriber email addresses.

Where it is stored

Subscriber details are stored in a restricted database hosted by Supabase. Visitors cannot view other subscribers or access this database directly. Access for maintenance is restricted to authorized service-side operations.

Preventing abuse

To keep the signup form working for everyone, we use short-lived rate-limit counters based on protected one-way identifiers. These may be derived from a visitor's network address, but we do not put raw visitor IP addresses into the newsletter subscriber table. Expired counters are removed during subsequent signup processing. Hosting providers may also process connection metadata in normal service logs.

Retention and withdrawal

We keep your address while you wish to receive newsletter updates. To unsubscribe, request a copy of your information, or ask us to delete your record, email dev.leonb@gmail.com. Unsubscribe requests are handled manually at present. If you withdraw consent, we will stop using your email for newsletter campaigns and process your deletion request as appropriate.

Important limits

Signing up records a request; the current form does not send a verification email or confirm ownership of the address. We will not treat an email address as proof of someone else's identity. Newsletter mail delivery, confirmation and automated unsubscribe links may be added later, with this notice updated accordingly.

Your choices

Newsletter signup is optional. You can decline without affecting your use of Droply. Where applicable, privacy law may give you further access, correction, erasure or complaint rights. Contact us at the address above to exercise them.
