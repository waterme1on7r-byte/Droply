Contact privacy.

Only the details needed to read and reply to your message.

Updated 18 November 2026 · This notice applies only to the marketing website contact form, not desktop file transfers.

What we collect

When you send a message, we collect the name, email address and message you provide, plus when it was submitted and its review status. This information is used to read your message and respond to you. It does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Where your message goes

Messages are stored in a protected Supabase database that the Droply team can review. They are not publicly searchable, and website visitors cannot read other people's messages. Sending a message does not automatically email a copy to an inbox.

Spam protection

We use short-lived rate-limiting counters derived from one-way protected identifiers. We do not store raw visitor IP addresses in the contact-message records, though hosting providers may process connection metadata in service logs.

Keeping and deleting messages

Contact messages are kept for handling your request and any necessary follow-up, and are deleted when no longer needed. If you want to review, correct or erase a message, contact dev.leonb@gmail.com. Please avoid sending passwords, financial data or other sensitive details through this form.

Your choices

Using this contact form is optional and does not affect the Droply desktop app. You may also contact us directly by email at the address above.
